Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

Cutaway Explained creates animated explainer videos. Cutaway Uploader ("the app") is our internal tool for publishing our own videos to our own social media accounts.

2. What data the app processes

The app is used only by the Cutaway Explained team. When we connect our own TikTok or YouTube account, the platform gives the app an access token and basic account information (such as the account's display name and ID). The app uses this only to upload and publish our videos and to confirm which account it is connected to.

The app does not collect data about other users or viewers, does not track visitors of this website, and does not use cookies or analytics.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the computer that runs the app and are never shared with third parties or sold. We can revoke the app's access at any time in the settings of the respective platform.

4. Third-party platforms

Videos are published through the official APIs of platforms such as TikTok and YouTube. These platforms process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Your rights

If you believe we hold personal data about you, you can contact us to request access or deletion. In practice the app does not store data about anyone other than our own accounts.
